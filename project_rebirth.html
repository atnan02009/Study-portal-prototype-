Tell the Consultant What's Happening





Consultant's Planning Framework

  1. Understand: identify the exact problem and ask follow-up questions if needed.
  2. Analyse: review study method, time, confidence, and practice evidence.
  3. Recommend: suggest a small, realistic action plan.
  4. Review: return after trying the plan and update the outcome.

Example Advice Card

Potential pattern: Understanding a lecture does not always mean you can retrieve and apply the idea independently.

Try next: close your notes, attempt a few questions, check mistakes, and revisit the difficult ones later.

Review: record how many you solved independently and update the related item in your Problem Vault.

This is example guidance, not a live AI response. Real AI consultation requires a secure backend connected to an AI service.

Problem Status Guide

  • Improving: progress is visible, but the student still needs practice.
